## 1. Executive summary

FoodApps Vietnam presents itself as a B2B service provider that helps restaurants and food businesses register, operate, and grow their stores on third-party food-delivery platforms. Its main proposition is to increase merchant revenue and improve online-store operations through onboarding support, menu and pricing setup, account management, advertising optimization, and training.

The website's primary focus is merchant enablement on platforms such as GrabFood and ShopeeFood. Its service page also lists BeFood and Xanh SM Ngon.

**Relevance to Xoài Go:** this is a potential merchant-enablement and operational-support model, not a consumer marketplace or a direct equivalent of Xoài Go's group-buying/community-discount proposition. Xoài can study the merchant acquisition funnel, onboarding support, account care, promotional operations, and performance reporting.

## 2. Company positioning and story

- Brand: FoodApps Việt Nam.
- Public-facing spokesperson/director named on the website: Nguyễn Mạnh Tuấn.
- The homepage states that the director has more than eight years of experience working at Grab and has supported restaurants on GrabFood.
- The company describes its role as a partner helping restaurants exploit online food-delivery channels and improve revenue.
- The stated approach emphasizes practical experience, platform data, understanding online customer behavior, and operational execution.
- The introduction page states a vision of becoming a leading provider of care, operations, and growth services for GrabFood and ShopeeFood stores in Vietnam.
- Its stated mission is to help Vietnamese food businesses digitize online sales, reach more customers, optimize operations, and grow sustainably.
- The stated culture is “Tự do trong trách nhiệm” (freedom with responsibility).

## 3. Services listed on the website

### 3.1 Store registration and onboarding

- Advise on required application documents.
- Prepare or improve food images and item descriptions.
- Set up the merchant store and menu.
- Advise on pricing.
- Guide merchants through activation and platform requirements.
- Platforms named: GrabFood, ShopeeFood, BeFood, and Xanh SM Ngon.

### 3.2 Store care and management

- Update menus, images, and promotions.
- Monitor store operations.
- Provide periodic revenue/performance reports.
- Help resolve store issues such as suspension, deactivation, or visibility errors.
- Manage stores across the platforms listed on the service page.

### 3.3 Advertising and revenue optimization

- Recommend advertising based on seasons and high-performing time windows.
- Optimize advertising budgets.
- Improve organic visibility.
- Measure results and adjust campaigns based on performance.

### 3.4 Training and growth solutions

- Training on selling through food-delivery apps.
- Practical resources and guidance for increasing sales.
- Longer-term growth advice tailored to each restaurant model.

## 4. Value proposition and operating principles

The website repeatedly emphasizes:

1. **One-to-one consultation:** advice tailored to the restaurant's operating model.
2. **End-to-end support:** registration, store care, advertising, and training.
3. **Operational tracking:** ongoing monitoring and periodic reporting focused on revenue outcomes.
4. **Data-informed decisions:** analyze platform, product, time-slot, and customer-behavior data.
5. **Practical execution:** package repeatable workflows while customizing them for different stores and restaurant groups.
6. **Online/offline distinction:** the introduction page notes that online and offline customers behave differently, so restaurants need a separate understanding of online demand.

## 8. What Xoài Go can learn and adapt

The following are product recommendations inferred from the public reference, not features claimed to exist in Xoài Go today.

### Merchant onboarding workflow

Create a lightweight merchant onboarding checklist:

1. Merchant profile and business category.
2. Address, contact, opening hours, and service area.
3. Product/menu catalog with clear photos and descriptions.
4. Pricing, promotions, and applicable conditions.
5. Verification/approval status.
6. Store activation and a first-week check-in.

### Merchant success operations

Consider a simple merchant success workspace with:

- Onboarding status and missing information.
- Menu/catalog completeness.
- Promotion schedule.
- Store activity and order/lead indicators.
- Support requests and issue status.
- Weekly merchant performance summary.

### Data and decision support

Where Xoài has legitimate access to its own transaction data, surface:

- Product/offer conversion.
- Active customers and repeat purchase.
- Orders by day and time.
- Promotion redemption.
- Merchant participation and retention.
- Customer feedback and complaint reasons.

Do not assume access to GrabFood/ShopeeFood internal data or attempt to collect data without authorization.

### Merchant-facing services to test

Xoài could test optional, clearly priced support services for partner merchants, such as:

- Setting up a merchant profile and offer catalogue.
- Producing better product photos/descriptions.
- Planning a weekly offer calendar.
- Helping a merchant design a group-buy offer.
- Reviewing Xoài campaign performance with the merchant.

These services should reinforce Xoài's core value: local customer community, partner discounts, and collective purchasing—not turn Xoài into a generic food-delivery-platform agency.

## 9. Suggested Xoài Go differentiation

| Dimension | FoodApps Vietnam public positioning | Suggested Xoài Go direction |
|---|---|---|
| Primary customer | Restaurant/food merchant | Local consumers and partner merchants |
| Core job | Grow third-party food-app stores | Generate local demand, member benefits, and group-buy volume |
| Distribution | GrabFood, ShopeeFood and other delivery apps | Xoài's own local community and partner network |
| Merchant value | Store setup, operation, advertising, training | New/repeat customers, discount campaigns, collective demand |
| Consumer proposition | Not the primary focus of the site | Membership benefits, nearby offers, group purchasing |
| Data loop | Store/platform performance | Member behavior, redemptions, group-buy demand, merchant outcomes |

## 10. Questions for Xoài's product team

- Should Xoài offer merchant onboarding as a free acquisition service?
- Which merchant fields and documents are essential for the first version?
- What minimum reporting would make a partner feel that Xoài is creating measurable value?
- Should group-buy campaigns have a merchant dashboard showing target quantity, committed quantity, deadline, and settlement status?
- Which merchant tasks can be self-service, and which require human support?
- How can Xoài measure incremental customers and repeat orders without claiming attribution it cannot prove?
